Internet sin líos

Internet sin líos is a Spanish-language educational channel of short videos that explains home internet and Wi-Fi to people without technical knowledge: one concept per video, an everyday comparison and one practical tip.

This page describes a personal publishing tool used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account (as drafts, using the TikTok Content Posting API). It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other users.
